We are Freshshop

FarmerStore is a web-based e-commerce platform developed using Python that connects sellers and buyers through a simple, secure, and user-friendly online marketplace. The primary objective of this project is to provide a digital platform where sellers can easily add, manage, and sell their products, while buyers can browse, purchase, and manage their orders conveniently. The system is designed to simplify the buying and selling process by bringing both sellers and customers together in a single online environment. With the rapid growth of digital technology and online shopping, businesses and individual sellers require efficient platforms to market their products and reach a wider audience. FarmerStore addresses this need by offering a reliable marketplace where products can be displayed, searched, and purchased with ease. The platform provides essential e-commerce functionalities such as product management, user authentication, order processing, and customer interaction. The project is developed using Python and modern web development technologies, making it scalable, secure, and easy to maintain. FarmerStore serves as an effective solution for online trading and demonstrates the practical implementation of web development concepts, database management, and user-centered design.

FarmerStore is designed with two primary user roles: Sellers and Buyers. Each role has its own set of functionalities and privileges within the system.
